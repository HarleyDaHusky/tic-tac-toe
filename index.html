✖

3️⃣ Choose game mode
4️⃣ Share ID with friend
5️⃣ Wait for opponent
6️⃣ Start playing!

🎯 Game Modes

Classic Mode

Traditional Challenge Tic-Tac-Toe

  • ❌ Player 1 is X
  • ⭕ Player 2 is O
  • 🏆 First to 3 in a row wins
  • 🤝 Draw if board fills
Crazy Mode

Challenge-Fill Mode

  • 📝 Phase 1: Fill 8 squares with challenges
  • ⚔️ Phase 2: Challenge squares
  • ✂️ Center = Rock Paper Scissors
  • 🎯 Winner of challenge claims square

🎯 How to Win

  • Classic Mode: Get three X's or O's in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally)
  • Crazy Mode:
    • First, fill the 8 outer squares with challenges (center is RPS)
    • Then challenge squares by clicking them
    • Challenge tiles: challenger chooses who wins that square
    • Center tile: play Rock Paper Scissors — both players pick, winner claims the square
    • First to get three symbols in a row wins!

🎨 Visual Guide

  • 🔴 Player 1: Red squares (X in Classic, words in Crazy)
  • 🔵 Player 2: Blue squares (O in Classic, words in Crazy)
  • 📘 Challenge Mode: Light blue cells during challenge fill
  • ✅ Selected: Green border shows selected cell

Motivation

  • The main motivation for "Crazy" mode was really to make a alternative mode to Tic-Tac-Toe where both players had the ability to set a list of different challenges/games/objectives to be able to determine a tile's winner.
  • This helps with making it so that there can be a complete stomp where there's only 1 color/shape/user on the board, or more stress to be applied due to needing to absolutely win a specific challenge.
  • I believe this is a fun alternative to classic Tic-Tac-Toe and can also be used to settle more fun disputes
  • I also wanted to make this project because it was a way for me to make a game that could be played between me and my partner in which we had always had games we were skill wise, better, worse, or equally matched in. But instead of just having a list and calculating points, now we have the chance to fight it out in Tic-Tac-Toe.

✨ Have fun and may the best player win! ✨
