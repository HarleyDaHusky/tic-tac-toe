🎮 How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe

✖

📋 Quick Start

1️⃣ Enter your name
2️⃣ Create/join with Game ID
3️⃣ Choose game mode
4️⃣ Share ID with friend
5️⃣ Wait for opponent
6️⃣ Start playing!

🎯 Game Modes

🎲 Classic Mode

Traditional Tic-Tac-Toe

  • ❌ Player 1 is X
  • ⭕ Player 2 is O
  • 🏆 First to 3 in a row wins
  • 🤝 Draw if board fills
🤪 Crazy Mode

Word-Fill Challenge

  • 📝 Phase 1: Fill 8 squares with words
  • ⚔️ Phase 2: Challenge squares
  • ✂️ Center = Rock Paper Scissors
  • 🎯 Winner of challenge claims square

🎯 How to Win

  • Classic Mode: Get three X's or O's in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally)
  • Crazy Mode:
    • First, fill the 8 outer squares with words (center is RPS)
    • Then challenge squares by clicking them
    • When challenging, you choose who wins that square
    • The winner gets their symbol (X/O) on that square
    • First to get three symbols in a row wins!

🎨 Visual Guide

  • 🔴 Player 1: Red squares (X in Classic, words in Crazy)
  • 🔵 Player 2: Blue squares (O in Classic, words in Crazy)
  • 📘 Word Mode: Light blue cells during word fill
  • ✅ Selected: Green border shows selected cell

⚡ Features

  • 🔄 Rematch: Click rematch after game ends
  • 👋 Leave Game: Close window or click X to forfeit
  • 📱 Responsive: Works on all devices
  • ⚡ Real-time: Instant moves with socket.io
  • 🏆 Forfeit Win: Win if opponent disconnects

✨ Have fun and may the best player win! ✨
